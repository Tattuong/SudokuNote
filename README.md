Practice faster, more accurate typing on your phone — for messages, study, and work.

Package: `com.SudokuNoteMNG.SudokuNote`

Remote config: [N277.json](https://api2.blwsmartware.net/N288.json) — `disable=1` hides Google Play Billing popups only. Shop stays open for coin unlocks.
