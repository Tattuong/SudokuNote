## Full description
SudokuNote is a phone typing trainer for messages, study, and work.

Run a 30 or 60 second test, or paste a paragraph you actually type. Errors are marked in the current sentence. After each run you see speed, accuracy, and the words you miss — then rebuild a drill from those mistakes. History and a personal speed goal stay on this device.

The system keyboard is used so Vietnamese Telex/VNI still works. Autocorrect and suggestions are off during a test so the phone does not rewrite your keys.

Looks, caret styles, 120-second tests, keep-screen-on, key click, and Pro history unlock with coins you earn by practicing or buy in 10 coin packs. Remove ads and SudokuNote Pro are one-time unlocks.

No account.
